# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Gluon-EVM is an open-source EVM smart-contract protocol developed under Stability Nexus.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users interact with the Gluon-EVM smart contracts, repository, deployment tooling, and related public blockchain infrastructure.

Gluon-EVM is designed as decentralized smart-contract software. The smart contracts do not operate a centralized user-account database and do not require users to provide names, email addresses, passwords, recovery phrases, or other traditional account information.

Interactions with Gluon-EVM occur on public blockchains. Information submitted to a blockchain may be publicly visible, permanent, and outside the control of the project maintainers.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gluon-EVM smart-contract project itself. Separate interfaces, wallets, RPC providers, blockchain explorers, hosting providers, oracle systems, or other third-party services used with Gluon-EVM may have their own privacy policies and practices.

## Information Processed by Gluon-EVM

The Gluon-EVM smart contracts may process public blockchain information required for protocol operation, including:

* Public blockchain wallet addresses
* Token and contract addresses
* Token balances and allowances
* Transaction inputs and calldata
* Reactor configuration values
* Oracle addresses and oracle values used by the protocol
* Treasury addresses and other public protocol configuration
* Transaction status, block data, and other information recorded by the blockchain
* Events emitted by Gluon-EVM smart contracts

This information is generally public blockchain data and may be visible to anyone who accesses the relevant blockchain.

The Gluon-EVM smart contracts do not request or store:

* Wallet passwords
* Wallet recovery phrases or seed phrases
* Private keys
* Email addresses
* Contacts
* Health information
* Precise device location
* Photos, files, microphone data, or camera data

Users authorize blockchain transactions through their own wallet software. Private keys and transaction signing remain under the control of the user's wallet.

## Repository and Development Information

The Gluon-EVM source code is hosted on GitHub.

When you access, clone, fork, contribute to, or otherwise interact with the GitHub repository, GitHub may process technical and account information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Public contributions may expose information associated with your GitHub account, including:

* GitHub username
* Public profile information
* Commit metadata
* Pull request and issue activity
* Public comments and reviews

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that they do not commit passwords, private keys, API secrets, personal information, or other sensitive material to the repository.

## Deployment and Development Tooling

Gluon-EVM includes development and deployment tooling intended for use by developers and operators.

Deployment scripts may read locally provided environment variables, such as a private key supplied by the operator for transaction signing.

The Gluon-EVM repository does not intentionally transmit or centrally store those private keys. Developers and operators are responsible for protecting their local environment, shell history, configuration files, wallets, and secrets.

Any RPC provider, wallet, node provider, or other infrastructure used to deploy or interact with Gluon-EVM may independently process technical request information according to its own policies.

## Public Blockchain Data

Blockchain transactions are public by design.

When you interact with Gluon-EVM, information associated with the transaction may be permanently recorded on the relevant blockchain, including:

* Your public wallet address
* Contract addresses
* Token transfers
* Transaction values
* Transaction calldata
* Protocol interactions
* Events emitted by smart contracts
* Block number and timestamp information

Public blockchain information may be analyzed, copied, indexed, or associated with other publicly available information by third parties.

The Gluon-EVM maintainers cannot delete, modify, or make private information that has already been confirmed on a public blockchain.

## Oracle and External Contract Data

Gluon-EVM can interact with external oracle contracts and other blockchain contracts through public interfaces.

Information read from or written to these contracts is part of the public blockchain environment.

External oracle systems, token contracts, and other protocols are independent systems and may have their own governance, security assumptions, data sources, and privacy practices.

Gluon-EVM does not control how independent third-party protocols process information outside the Gluon-EVM contracts.

## Data Storage

The Gluon-EVM smart contracts do not maintain a centralized off-chain database of user information.

Protocol state is stored on the relevant public blockchain as smart-contract storage, transaction history, and emitted events.

Because blockchain data is generally permanent and replicated across many independent nodes, confirmed blockchain information usually cannot be deleted by the Gluon-EVM maintainers.

Local development files, environment variables, wallet information, and other information stored on a user's device are controlled by that user and by the software they choose to use.

## Data Sharing

Gluon-EVM does not sell personal information.

The project does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

Public blockchain interactions are inherently shared with the blockchain network. Information submitted through a transaction may be received, processed, stored, indexed, or displayed by:

* Blockchain validators and nodes
* RPC providers
* Wallet providers
* Oracle systems
* Token contracts and other smart contracts
* Blockchain explorers
* Analytics and indexing services

These third parties operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies, terms, and technical architectures.

## Data Security

Gluon-EVM aims to reduce privacy and security risk by using transparent smart contracts and by avoiding centralized storage of user credentials or private keys.

However, no blockchain system, software repository, wallet, RPC provider, or electronic communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users and developers are responsible for protecting:

* Private keys
* Recovery phrases
* Wallet passwords
* Devices
* Development environments
* Environment variables and secrets
* Transaction signing permissions

Users should review transaction details, contract addresses, token approvals, network selection, and other relevant information before signing blockchain transactions.

## Data Deletion

The Gluon-EVM project does not maintain a centralized user database from which personal information can be deleted.

Local information stored by wallets, development tools, terminals, browsers, or other software may generally be removed using the controls provided by those tools.

Information confirmed on a public blockchain generally cannot be modified or deleted. Transaction history, wallet addresses, contract interactions, and emitted events may remain permanently available.

Information independently processed by GitHub, RPC providers, wallets, blockchain explorers, or other third parties is subject to those providers' deletion policies.

## Data Export

Gluon-EVM does not maintain centralized user profiles or a private collection of personal information for export.

Public blockchain information can be independently accessed using blockchain nodes, RPC providers, wallets, explorers, indexing services, and other compatible tools.

Repository contribution data can be accessed through GitHub according to GitHub's available tools and policies.

## Children's Privacy

Gluon-EVM does not intentionally request age information or information specifically concerning children.

Because Gluon-EVM is open-source blockchain software, users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the protocol and related tools complies with the laws and requirements applicable to them.

## Open-Source Access and Blockchain Fees

The Gluon-EVM source code is publicly available as open-source software.

Using or deploying the smart contracts may require blockchain transaction fees, commonly referred to as gas fees.

Protocol interactions may also involve fees defined by the applicable smart contracts.

Blockchain gas fees and protocol-level fees are separate from access to the open-source repository itself.

## Third-Party Services

Users may interact with Gluon-EVM through third-party services such as:

* Wallet software
* RPC and node providers
* Blockchain explorers
* Oracle systems
* GitHub
* Frontend applications, including the Gluon-EVM WebUI or other interfaces

Those services are not governed by this Privacy Policy and may independently collect or process information.

Users should review the privacy policies and terms of the third-party services they choose to use.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Gluon-EVM, its documentation, supported infrastructure, or applicable requirements.

Updates will be published in the Gluon-EVM repository.
